# 📊 Sales Performance Analysis | Google Sheets

## 🔍 Project Overview
This project analyzes business performance using **Google Sheets**.  
The analysis focuses on sales, profit, margin, product categories, regions, sales channels, delivery time, and **ABC analysis** based on the Pareto principle.

The goal was to identify the main profit drivers, compare online vs offline performance, evaluate geographic differences, and support business decision-making with structured spreadsheet analysis.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Skills Used
- **Google Sheets**
- Pivot tables
- Formulas and calculated metrics
- Data cleaning
- Business analysis
- ABC analysis
- Data visualization

---

## 📈 Key Metrics
- **Total orders:** 1,248
- **Total profit:** $473,961,408.74
- **Countries covered:** 45

---

## 📊 What I Analyzed

### Product Category Performance
- Identified the most popular and most profitable product categories
- Compared categories by revenue and profit contribution
- Highlighted low-performing categories for further review

### Geographic Analysis
- Analyzed sales and profit across countries and regions
- Identified top-performing countries and the most profitable regions
- Compared regional differences in business performance

### Sales Channels
- Compared **online vs offline** sales performance
- Measured channel contribution to total sales and profit
- Identified the profit gap between sales channels

### Delivery Performance
- Calculated average delivery time
- Identified categories and countries with the longest shipping delays
- Checked whether delivery time had a visible relationship with profit

### Time Trends
- Analyzed sales dynamics by month and weekday
- Identified peak and low sales periods
- Highlighted the most profitable weekday

### ABC Analysis
- Segmented product categories into **A / B / C groups**
- Identified categories generating the majority of profit
- Applied the Pareto principle to support prioritization

---

## 🔥 Key Insights
- **Office Supplies** was the most popular category
- **Cosmetics** was the most profitable category
- **Fruits** was the least profitable category
- **Offline sales** slightly outperformed online sales in profit
- **Southern and Eastern Europe** showed the strongest regional performance
- No strong relationship was observed between delivery time and profit
- A relatively small group of categories generated the majority of total profit

---

## 🧠 Business Recommendations
- Improve online channel performance through optimization and targeted campaigns
- Focus on high-profit categories such as **Cosmetics** and **Office Supplies**
- Review low-performing categories and optimize pricing/logistics
- Strengthen business presence in top-performing countries and regions
- Use seasonal patterns and weekday trends for better planning
- Continue monitoring delivery efficiency and customer experience
